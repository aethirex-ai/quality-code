# AethDesign: richer portfolios with cheaper implementation owners

**12 October 2026 · one paired workload · two fictional clients per subject**

Cheaper coherent owners kept the speed advantage and reduced the coding-token price estimate in this pair: **40.5% less elapsed time and 23.9% lower hypothetical coding API cost**, including builder corrections. Aggregate input rose **291.1%** and output **73.2%**. The single-agent designs scored higher in anonymous review, and the adaptive trip builder retained one independently reproduced persistence defect. This is a measured trade-off, not a general quality or subscription-saving claim.

[Explore all four frozen client experiences](https://aethirex-ai.github.io/quality-code/benchmarks/rich-client-pilot-2026-10/). Each has three pages; these are richer portfolio concepts rather than one-page local-business templates.

## The workload and routing

**AUREL** is an architectural lighting showroom, editorial collection and persistent specification planner. It includes terrain-free product filters, accessible product dialogs, integer-cent estimates, editable quantities, removal, corrupted-state recovery and a local project form. **MERIDIAN** is an expedition journal, filtered journey catalogue and trip builder with changing itineraries, traveler bounds, guide pricing, persistent choices and a local enquiry note. All content is fictional; no message, payment or booking is sent.

Both conditions started from identical fixtures, used the same native root (`gpt-6.1-sol`, high effort), had a 35-minute shared allowance and produced four native photographs. Single ran first. Adaptive used two direct complete-client owners, both native-discovered `gpt-6-luna` at high effort, with fresh scoped histories and 2,660/2,572-character briefs. The primary generated and inspected images while owners implemented, then integrated and inspected the experience. There were no nested owners or model escalations. Observed names/settings describe this experiment; Quality Code remains provider-neutral. The available catalog and rates support the selection, not a claim of a perfect model match.

## Iterations and output errors

A correction cycle requires an observed issue, a correction and a targeted successful recheck. Check reruns, model turns, initial scaffolding and expected negative tests are not additional defects. Self-reported logs were reconciled with native traces and screenshots by a fresh independent reviewer. Counts are observed lower bounds; some early adaptive tool failures were not durably captured.

| Observed evidence | Single | Cheaper owners |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Recorded builder check runs | 51 | 36 |
| Nonzero records, before classification | 8 | 6 |
| Expected initial scaffold failures | 1 | 1 |
| Unexpected nonzero recorded checks | 7 | 2 |
| Distinct cause types with evidenced correction/recheck | 8 | 8 |
| Confirmed contract/product or visual corrections | 2 | 3 |
| Additional mobile automation reachability correction | 1, manual impact uncertain | 0 confirmed |
| Independently reproduced remaining functional causes | 0 observed; form check unresolved | 1 |

Single corrected a duplicate required add-button selector and a misplaced route graphic. A mobile guide-action timeout also received a correction, but manual user impact is uncertain. Five remaining correction causes were QA/tooling mistakes. Eight cause cycles are therefore not eight user-facing bugs.

Adaptive corrected dead home-page actions, prototype storage identifiers and a fixture drawing/dialog mismatch. Nine issue-ID correction sequences reduce to eight cause types because two owners fixed the same malformed preview-key field separately. A claimed mobile overflow was withdrawn after the screenshots/diagnostics contradicted it. A wrong-interpreter failure lacks a targeted later recheck and is excluded from correction cycles. Its six nonzero check records include one scaffold failure, two expected recorder-negative subprocesses, one intentional missing-command reproduction and two genuinely malformed preview requests. Early unrecorded errors mean the smaller recorded failure count does not prove greater reliability.

The remaining adaptive defect is concrete: enter the builder through `?trip=ridge`, select FJORD for two travelers with a guide (€4,200), then reload. It re-applies the URL's RIDGE choice, changes the estimate to €5,400 and overwrites the saved selection. A healthy control entering the bare builder URL retains FJORD. One cause produces the persistence failure and four downstream boundary assertions. No measured output was fixed after freezing. [Independent iteration review](iteration-review.json) and [browser reproduction/control](browser-diagnosis.json) retain the distinctions.

This workload measures construction and correction quality. The [previous isolated error-finding comparison](../client-pilot-round2-2026-10/RESULTS.md#independent-error-finding-workload) remains separate; this is not a new seeded-defect recall experiment.

## External evaluation and its limits

The original preflight-audited evaluator omitted `.mjs` from its local server MIME map. It returned 97/142 for single and 129/142 for adaptive. The control's modules could not load under that server, creating cascading failures. Both original results remain published; they are unsuitable as an unqualified quality comparison.

A documented **MIME-only repair** served modules as JavaScript and checked both unchanged frozen outputs again, yielding 141/142 and 130/142. No selectors, acceptance assertions, timeout or website source changed. The [amendment](evaluation-amendment.json) retains both evaluator hashes, and both versions are published.

The adaptive corrected failures include six assertions requiring photographic `<img>` content on every page, one URL matcher that rejects a legitimate query string, and five manifestations of the confirmed persistence defect. Missing photography on utility/catalogue pages is a requirement-interpretation/presentation distinction, not a broken resource. The single form assertion failed, but the independent targeted browser sequence submitted successfully and rendered markup as plain text; no corresponding product defect was reproduced. The original adaptive form timeout also passed in the corrected run. The 800ms action deadline and exact matchers limit the numerical scores. We retain them and disclose diagnosis rather than adjusting assertions to get a preferred outcome.

The 142 probes sample six pages at 375px/1440px and local interaction contracts. They do not prove complete accessibility, security, intermediate-width behavior or production readiness. Test counts are not distinct-defect counts.

## Images, reproducibility and interpretation

Each subject generated four originals in a literal parallel job list with four successful native returns and no observed retry. The general telemetry parser correctly keeps `image_execution_ambiguous` and its call count unknown. Separate [single](single-image-transcript-supplement.json) and [adaptive](swarm-image-transcript-supplement.json) supplements verify four calls/returns and delivered-original hashes. Backend identity, generator tokens and charges remain unavailable; no specific image-model claim is made.

The result is **faster and lower-priced coding, with a visible quality and correctness trade-off** in one pair. It supports coherent ownership and measuring repair costs. It does not justify automatic recursion, perfect-fit model claims or assuming cheaper implementation is equally reliable. Keep state/persistence boundaries in primary integration checks and escalate on concrete failures; risk gates still apply.

Order, stochastic imagery, service/cache conditions, model and implementation topology differ. A repeated counterbalanced ablation would be needed for causal instruction/model conclusions. Frozen outputs and all failures are retained; no best-of rerun or post-grading repair was selected. General Quality Code policy stays provider-neutral. Public replay sources normalize host paths, retaining original protocol and source-provenance hashes; raw native transcripts and session identities stay private.
