# Fixture verification
## Test objectives
Three usable distinct professional fictional portfolio landing pages, local generated imagery, functional demonstration CTAs, responsive layout, accessible semantics, no broken resources.
## Product risks
Normal: local static UI with demo-only state, no production data, backend or deployment. Evaluation cannot establish business conversion rates.
## Test levels
Builder checks plus external browser checks at desktop and mobile sizes. Independent anonymized visual review occurs after the timed attempt.
## Test data and environment
Synthetic businesses only. Dependency-free static site; native image tool, installed Node/Playwright available through BRIEF.md path.
## Known gaps and residual risk
Image-model identity and image-call token usage are not exposed by the native tool. Shared-host load and provider caching are uncontrolled. A single paired workload does not establish general superiority.
