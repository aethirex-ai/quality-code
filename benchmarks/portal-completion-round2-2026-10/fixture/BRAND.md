AethDesign is an independent software development and UI/UX/web/app studio. It connects product strategy, interface systems, responsive frontend, backend architecture, APIs/data, practical AI implementation, privacy-conscious engineering, hosting and launch. Its message is digital products built end to end. The real studio is not an atelier, crafts studio or graphic-design-only agency. This portal is a fictional client-work demonstration for that real studio; all customers, accounts, budgets and project data are synthetic. Do not invent real customer claims, certifications or outcomes.

Observed landing-page identity: near-black canvas, silver/off-white text, fine restrained borders, generous purposeful spacing, quiet motion/texture, serif display headings and clear sans-serif body/navigation. Local source style references use #0A0A0A background, #C0C0C0 text, brighter off-white emphasis, DM Sans/system sans body and Cormorant Garamond/Georgia display. Font files are not provided; choose local fallbacks rather than external loading. Translate the identity into an efficient product interface, not a copy of a marketing hero on every screen. A limited accessible accent for state/action is permitted. Maintain one consistent system of type, spacing, panels, navigation and control states across client/studio views.

Portfolio purpose: demonstrate software delivery and UI/UX capability through a believable working product. Prioritize useful task hierarchy, realistic SaaS/commerce interface previews, readable investment/milestone/revision summaries, understandable recovery states and an intentional mobile reading order. Use code-native UI artwork where relevant; ornamental identity/editorial illustrations are not the product story. Selected project information should precede its decisions/publishing controls on mobile. Do not let repeated giant hero art push every workflow down the page.
