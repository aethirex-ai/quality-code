03 / FROM GROUND TO GROWTH

A considered process.

Clear steps. Shared decisions.
Space for the unexpected.

01

Read the site.

We start with your brief, a site walk and the practical questions: light, ground, access, use and what you want to keep.

02

Shape the idea.

Sketches and a concept plan make the possibilities visible. Together, we refine the layout, material palette and planting approach.

03

Bring it to life.

Detailed plans support the build and planting. We agree the scope of site coordination and ongoing care before work begins.

04 / BEGIN WITH A CONVERSATION

What could
grow here?

Tell us a little about the place you have in mind. A garden, a courtyard, a shared corner of the city.

Explore the local enquiry demo. Nothing is sent or saved, and no estimate or appointment is created.
