01 — THE HOUSE

Nothing to rush.
Everything to savour.

At the end of a winding coastal lane, TIDE HOUSE opens onto the sheltered waters of Alder Cove. A small, imagined hideaway for long walks, good books and mornings measured in cups of coffee.

Come for the view. Settle in for the sea-facing breakfast room, the library’s well-worn armchairs, and the simple pleasure of having nowhere else to be.
