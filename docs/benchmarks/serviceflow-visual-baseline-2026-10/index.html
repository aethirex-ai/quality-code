ServiceFlow frontend remake trial

Two existing apps. One instruction: Fully remake the frontend of this ServiceFlow app.

The agents received the installed Quality Visuals baseline and factual app context. No layout, reference design or list of desired fixes was supplied. Originals and first reported handoffs were frozen before parent-provided audit feedback. The adaptive run could choose helpers, including reviewers, inside its one-prompt workflow.

Method and limits

The old benchmark remains available with its original results. This trial does not revise those scores. The two apps have different starting implementations, and there is one run per app. Changes within each app are observational; they do not establish a general effect of the baseline or a causal advantage for adaptive delegation.

Screenshot presentation can clip verified empty capture-canvas margins caused by browser zoom. Raw files are linked beside each view and remain byte-exact. Missing evidence is identified rather than treated as a passing check.

Ownership and data

ServiceFlow and its home-services businesses are fictional demonstrations. AethDesign is the software and UI/UX studio behind this experiment. No customer relationship or completed service job is claimed.

This read-only gallery contains static screenshots and recorded results. It has no demo login, enquiry form, analytics integration or connection to the local app databases.
