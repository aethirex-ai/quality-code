# Why the ServiceFlow remake still felt generic

AethDesign owner feedback, 2026-10-15: the frontends improved from roughly 5/10 to 6.5/10, with fewer generic markers but many remaining. This is an attributed qualitative assessment, not a new independent score, measured benchmark result or replacement verdict. The [frozen comparison](benchmarks/serviceflow-visual-baseline-2026-10/index.html) and its [method and source projections](../benchmarks/serviceflow-visual-baseline-2026-10/README.md) remain unchanged.

## What the records show

Both initial builder reports foregrounded cream/ivory, terracotta, typography and imagery. The public before/first/final views show real improvements: removed reassurance/closing filler, photography and more coherent task screens. They also retain a split hero, service collection and three-step process. Those familiar patterns are not automatically defects. The available evidence does not demonstrate why that hierarchy was the best result of a full remake.

The first adaptive completed audit reported PASS with no findings, before a supplemental content review identified five defect families. Single review caught some duplicate labels and layout issues but missed broader redundant copy. The [retained supplemental findings](../benchmarks/serviceflow-visual-baseline-2026-10/reviews/supplemental-content.json) include repetitive supporting text and inaccurate workflow/privacy assurances. Later reviews verified their corrections. A subsequent shared-account privacy correction and its history are also retained in the comparison. Historical findings are not asserted to remain unresolved in the current apps.

| Gap | Evidence and limit | Rule change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Styling decisions substituted for an explicit structural design basis | Builder reports and inherited page order support this inference; they do not reveal all internal reasoning | Establish audience/tasks, information priority and composition before styling; explain retained structures |
| A full remake was not judged separately from cleanup | Real visual changes occurred, while the major landing-page pattern remained | Review remake intent against the original request and original/final renders, independently of functionality |
| Individual patterns could be justified too easily | A useful catalogue or actual process can be valid while the cumulative page remains formulaic | Inspect the complete composition and each repeated treatment's contribution in context |
| Content scrutiny lagged functional scrutiny | Subsequent audits found redundant/inaccurate copy after earlier acceptance | Record explicit content/composition evidence alongside interaction and product checks |
| Focused correction gates could be mistaken for broad design endorsement | Re-reviews addressed their stated fixes, not proof of a newly conceived design | Preserve scope and prior verdicts; inspect each fix's full-page effects without claiming broader certification |

## What a full remake now requires

The [canonical visual module](../assets/modules/visuals.md) defines the builder obligations. The [visual audit reference](../references/VISUAL_AUDIT.md) defines independent comparison and evidence. Agents must reconsider the frontend while preserving product contracts, rather than treating existing markup and section order as requirements. A short internal design basis is enough; the user need not provide a layout prescription.

The rules do not require novelty for its own sake, a different layout for every screen, extra features, arbitrary percentages of changed pixels, a numerical quality threshold or removal of every card, icon or numbered sequence. Reviewers must connect failures to product needs, requested scope or observable baseline requirements. Taste alone does not establish a defect, and a pleasing image does not clear an unmet requirement.

## Remaining uncertainty

No new app trial was run for this policy update. Instruction delivery and independent scenario review can check coherence and obvious loopholes, but cannot establish future model compliance, better aesthetics, speed or savings. The next app experiment must freeze these revised instructions and distinguish first handoff from correction workflows again.
